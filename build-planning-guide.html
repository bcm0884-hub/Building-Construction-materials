Simba Cement · Build planning checklist

Before you order materials

Take this list to your project engineer, contractor or supplier. Project requirements vary, so use professional guidance for specifications and quantities.

Project information

  • ☐ Project location and delivery access
  • ☐ Type of work and construction stage
  • ☐ Project drawings, structural design or bill of quantities
  • ☐ Required cement grade and applicable technical standards, as specified by your engineer
  • ☐ Estimated amount and expected delivery schedule

Questions for your supplier

  • ☐ Which Simba Cement grade matches my project specification?
  • ☐ What are the current product documents and handling instructions?
  • ☐ Is the product in stock, and what are the delivery options?
  • ☐ How should cement be stored and protected on site?
  • ☐ What are the current price, payment terms and invoice details?
This checklist is a general planning aid. It is not a cement mix design, product data sheet, engineering recommendation or safety manual. Follow current manufacturer instructions and professional advice.
