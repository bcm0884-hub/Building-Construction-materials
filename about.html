Our story · Our roots

Building the nation since 2010.

Simba Cement is made by National Cement Company Limited, a Kenyan manufacturer and member of Devki Group.

Quality changes the nation.

National Cement Company Limited began producing and supplying cement under the Simba Cement brand in 2010. The company is part of Devki Group and manufactures cement and paving blocks for customers across the country.

As demand for Simba Cement grew, the company expanded its grinding capacity fivefold in 2013. It developed clinker manufacturing in Merueshi, Kenya, to use local resources, and expanded its reach into neighboring East African countries.

Made for people building the future

From homes and businesses to roads and community infrastructure, quality building materials play a role in the places we depend on. The Simba Cement range includes Simba Cement 32.5R and Simba Power 42.5N for different construction applications.
