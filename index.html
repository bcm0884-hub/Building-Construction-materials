Builder tips

Get more from every bag.

Tip 01

Keep bags dry and off the floor

Store cement on pallets in a covered, dry place, away from walls, so moisture can’t reach it.

Tip 02

First in, first out

Use older bags first. Cement is best used fresh, so plan deliveries around your build schedule.

Tip 03

Measure water carefully

Too much water weakens concrete. Add just enough for a workable mix.

Tip 04

Cure for at least 7 days

Keep fresh concrete damp by spraying or covering it, so it gains full strength.

Tip 05

Use clean sand and ballast

Dirt and clay in aggregates reduce strength. Wash or screen them before mixing.

Tip 06

Protect your skin and eyes

Wear gloves, boots and goggles. Wash off wet cement quickly to avoid burns.

What is the difference between 32.5R and 42.5N?

The number is the strength class: the minimum compressive strength in megapascals at 28 days. 42.5N is stronger and is used for specialized, heavy-load work. 32.5R is a general-purpose cement, and the “R” means rapid early strength.

Which grade should I use for my house?

Most home building, including foundations, walls, slabs and plaster, uses a general-purpose cement like Simba Cement 32.5R. For suspended slabs, columns or multi-storey work, follow your engineer’s specification.

How long can I store cement?

Cement is best used within about three months of packing. Keep it dry, covered and off the ground, and use older bags first.

Handle with care

Handling & storage

  • Store bags on pallets in a dry, covered area, stacked no more than 10 high.
  • Keep bags away from walls and off damp floors.
  • Use older stock first and reseal opened bags tightly.
  • Wear gloves, long sleeves, boots and eye protection when mixing.
  • If wet cement touches your skin or eyes, rinse with plenty of clean water.
  • Avoid breathing in cement dust. Mix in a ventilated area or wear a dust mask.
Always read the handling instructions printed on the bag.
