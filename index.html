FAQ

Common Questions About Our Materials

Find answers about materials, delivery, pricing, and ordering.

What cement brands do you supply?

We supply premium cement brands including Simba, Bamburi, Rai, and Blue Triangle. All are OPC cement suitable for general construction.

What steel bar sizes are available?

We stock D8, D10, D12, and D16 steel bars in Grade 60. All bars are 12m lengths. Custom orders available upon request.

Do you offer delivery to my site?

Yes! We deliver to sites across Kenya. Delivery rates depend on location and quantity. Contact us for a custom delivery quote.

Do you offer bulk discounts?

Absolutely! We offer competitive bulk discounts for contractors and large projects. Get in touch with your project details for pricing.

What water tank sizes do you have?

We stock water tanks from 500L to 10,000L capacity in both plastic and metal. We also help with installation advice.

How can I request a quote?

Call +254 726 866 998, WhatsApp us, or fill out our quote request form. We'll respond within 24 hours.
